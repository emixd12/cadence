## Findings

- **F1. Timezone change stalls account sync when an archived Behavior exists.**
  `apply_account_sync_plan` requires a timezone upsert for every Behavior, archived
  included, when the profile timezone changes. This rule exists since
  `20260901054332` and is unchanged by the contract migration. Desktop
  `local_store/behavior.rs::update_timezone` (unchanged since 0.1.5) and the web RPC
  update only active Behaviors. On 0.1.5 the plan failed with "A profile timezone change
  requires every Behavior timezone write." until the change was reverted. 0.1.6 has the
  same behavior; it is not a regression.
- **F2. Account sync and location reads stall while the window is hidden or occluded.**
  WebKit reported the window occluded (on another Space, behind other windows, or under
  the computer-use screen overlay). `document.hidden` then blocked location reads, and one
  **Sync now** took 3 min 23 s until the window was visible. Visible syncs took 10 s on
  the small test account and 55 s to 1 min 47 s on the owner's account.
- **F3. Completion labels depend on a fresh CoreLocation fix.** The marker read succeeded
  at 21:37:14Z. Both completion-time reads failed (21:44:06Z `timeout`, 21:46:06Z
  `cl_error_0`), as did a travel refresh read at 21:45:14Z (`cl_error_0`). The app saved
  the completion without a label and showed no error, which is the specified behavior.
