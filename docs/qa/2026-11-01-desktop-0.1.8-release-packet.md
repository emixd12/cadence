## Limitations and rollback

- To stop further installs, restore the saved feeds from
  `acceptance-0.1.8/publish/rollback/`; installed copies have no downgrade path. To roll
  back the download link, copy `Cadence_0.1.6_aarch64.dmg` (`3feecc5e…`, still published)
  to `Cadence_aarch64.dmg` the same way.
- The test Mac now runs 0.1.8 from `/Applications`, linked to the test account (session
  `b90256fd…`). Its 0.1.6 rollback copies are in `backups/pre-0.1.8-20261102T062223Z/`.
- The 0.1.6 → 0.1.8 path through the updater was not exercised before publication;
  check 4 applies to the next release.
- Carried forward from the [0.1.6 packet](2026-10-27-desktop-0.1.6-release-packet.md): an
  installed completion place label, installed receipt replay, and the F1 timezone fix
  confirmed from an installed Mac are not established. macOS 14 is not tested.
- `desktop-release-acceptance.mjs --same-schema` cannot pass once a launch sync has run;
  0.1.7 used a classified per-table comparison instead
  ([0.1.7 packet](2026-11-01-desktop-0.1.7-release-packet.md#upgrade-comparison)).
