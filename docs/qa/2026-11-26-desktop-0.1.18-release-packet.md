## Release notes

> Cadence 0.1.18 fixes Connect Google Calendar for AI on the Mac. In 0.1.17, pressing it showed "Google Calendar for AI could not start connecting." Now it opens Google in your browser to finish connecting. Signing out also clears any unfinished AI Calendar connection. Your data and settings carry over unchanged.
>
> Cadence for Mac needs an Apple Silicon Mac. It has been tested on macOS 27.
