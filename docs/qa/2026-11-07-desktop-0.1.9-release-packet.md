## Limitations and rollback

- To stop further installs, restore the saved feeds from `acceptance-0.1.9/publish/rollback/`;
  installed copies have no downgrade path. To roll back the download link, copy
  `Cadence_0.1.8_aarch64.dmg` (`b78c3e68…`, still published) to `Cadence_aarch64.dmg` the same
  way.
- Published 0.1.8 copies with an auto-archived Behavior open blank and cannot reach Settings, so
  their updater cannot install 0.1.9; those users need the download. The release body says so.
- Check 4 ran on the owner's Mac, not the test Mac, and the 0.1.8 there was installed over
  0.1.10-dev.1 data (same schema 20) for the check.
- The test Mac now runs 0.1.9 from `/Applications`, linked to account A (session `a8fdcaf5…`).
  Its 0.1.8 rollback copies are in `backups/pre-0.1.9-20261107T211757Z/`.
- Not established: activation from a leave reminder into a Calendar event's drawer on an
  installed Mac (Ticket 285). macOS 14 is not tested.
- `desktop-release-acceptance.mjs --same-schema` still cannot pass once a launch sync has run;
  the classified per-table comparison stands in, as for 0.1.7 and 0.1.8.

Platform impact: desktop release only; web, marketing and future mobile have no change (the
marketing download link points at the version-free asset). Messaging impact: none new; the
release body's blank-window line is release mechanics, and `P-027` covers the recovery wording.
