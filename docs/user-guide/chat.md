# Chat

**Chat** talks to Cadence in plain words: ask what is on today, what you skipped
this week, or ask it to mark, note or move an item. It answers about your own
Cadence records and connected calendars. Chat is web only for now and is not yet
in production.

## Ask a question

Open **Chat** in the navigation. A new conversation opens with three questions to
try. They come from your own week: a Behavior that keeps slipping, items still
waiting for a mark, or what is left today. With little to go on, you see three
general questions instead. Choose one, or type in the field at the bottom and press **Send** (or Enter;
Shift+Enter starts a new line). Chat can read all of your Cadence data, including
notes and connected calendars; **Change this in Settings** opens Settings.

While it works, small grey lines say what it looked at, such as "Looked at your
Timeline". If a source is off in Settings, the line says so and Chat does not
read it. A medical question gets no advice; a small grey note under the reply
reads "Cadence does not advise on medical matters." Chat still says where the
item fits in your day.

While Chat answers, **Send** reads **Stop**. Choose it (or press Escape in the
field) to end the answer early. What Chat already wrote stays, and changes it
already made keep their **Undo**; the answer ends with "Stopped." Changes still
waiting for a Yes are dropped.

If you scroll up while Chat answers, the page stays where you are and **Jump to
latest** appears above the field.

If a message never reached Cadence, it goes back into the field with one red
sentence above it; send it again when you are ready. If the connection drops in
the middle of an answer, what arrived stays and **Send again** asks once more.
Cadence never runs the same message twice.

Reloading the page reopens the conversation you had open.

## Undo a change

When Chat changes one record, the change is written in italics under the reply,
for example "Marked Meds done." Choose **Undo** next to it to put it back. A
change that cannot be undone, such as a Behavior edit, has no **Undo**.

## Answer Yes or No

Behavior changes, and a second change in the same question, wait for you. Chat
lists every change it would make with **Yes** · **No**. **Yes** makes them all
and lists what ran; **No** changes nothing. The question expires after 15
minutes, when you send another message, or when one of the records changed.

## When a change is refused

A change Chat cannot make shows as one red sentence starting "Not changed", for
example when the new time has an event on your calendar. Up to three free times
follow; choosing one asks Chat to move the item there.

## Past conversations

Choose **Past conversations** to see your conversations, newest first, and open
one to continue it. **New conversation** starts a fresh one.

Your most recent chats are also in the side menu. Up to five of them show under
**Chat**, with **All conversations** at the end. Choose the small arrow next to
**Chat** to hide them, and again to show them. The menu remembers which you picked. Conversations are
kept until you delete your data in Settings.
